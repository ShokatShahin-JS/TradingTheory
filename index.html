Instrument & settings

Changing the starting balance updates the open backtest straight away. Profit is in the quote currency (USD for gold). Charts are bid prices: buys fill at bid + spread, and shorts exit at the ask.

Getting data from MT5: View → Symbols → Bars tab → pick the symbol and timeframe → Request → Export Bars. The tab-separated <DATE> <TIME> <OPEN>… file loads as is. TradingView and Dukascopy exports with time, open, high, low, close columns also work. Use M1 or M5 data so higher timeframes build up candle by candle.

Trade the live market

Paper trading on real-time prices with your account, chart tools and indicators. Your live trades save like a backtest.

Live data works in the phone app and the offline file. This version inside Claude can't connect to market-data sites. Open Bar-Replay-Offline.html or your installed phone app to go live.

Get a free key at twelvedata.com → Sign up → API keys. It stays on this device. The free plan allows 800 requests a day, so prices refresh every .

Crypto comes from Binance, tick by tick, with no account needed. PAX Gold (PAXG) is a token backed by gold and tracks the gold price around the clock, weekends included. The gold and silver perpetuals also stream the real order book. Footprint, heatmap, book and delta are recorded while you are live and saved on this device for replay.
